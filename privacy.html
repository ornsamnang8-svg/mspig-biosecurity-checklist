Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive only your name, email address and profile picture. We store the biosecurity audit answers, farm details, signatures and saved records (up to 8) that you enter in the app.

How we use it

Data is used only to run farm biosecurity audits and reports. Each user sees only their own data; only the company administrator can view all data for oversight. We do not sell or share data with third parties.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored securely on Supabase (Singapore). To delete your account or data, contact orn.samnang8@gmail.com.

Terms of use

The app is provided for internal farm biosecurity work of M'S PIG ACMC (CAMBODIA) CO., LTD. and its partner farms. Please enter accurate information and keep your Google account secure.
